V29 Steam-side online menu patch

What I found in the custom Steam files:
- The online bypass hooks are installing, but the Steam emulation can still affect the menu because NSC depends on Steam callbacks/call-results for stats, auth tickets, leaderboard, and lobby/matchmaking state.
- Important callbacks like UserStatsReceived, LeaderboardFindResult, LobbyMatchList, LobbyCreated, LobbyEnter, and AuthTicket are one-shot messages. If the game registers/polls late, the result can be missed and the menu state can fall back to online-error/locked flow.

What changed:
- SteamCallbackManager now keeps sticky copies of important online callbacks.
- When NSC registers a callback late, the latest sticky result is replayed.
- SteamCallResultManager mirrors more online-menu call results as callbacks too.
- SteamAPI_RegisterCallback and SteamAPI_RegisterCallResult flush callbacks immediately once after registration.
- Kept startup popup, F2 runtime menu, and multi-popup behavior intact.
